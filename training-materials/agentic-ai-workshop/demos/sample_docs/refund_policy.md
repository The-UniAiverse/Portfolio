# Acme Logistics — Customer Refund & Claims Policy (v2, effective 1 January 2026)

## 1. Scope
Applies to all domestic shipments booked through the Acme portal or API. International shipments are governed by the carrier's terms.

## 2. Late delivery
If a shipment with a guaranteed delivery date arrives more than 24 hours late, the customer is entitled to a refund of the shipping fee. Refunds are processed within 7 working days to the original payment method. Claims must be raised within 14 days of the delivery date.

## 3. Damaged goods
Damage claims require photographs and the packing slip, submitted within 48 hours of delivery. Approved claims are reimbursed at declared value up to INR 50,000 unless additional insurance was purchased. Claims above INR 10,000 are reviewed by the Claims Committee, which meets weekly.

## 4. Lost shipments
A shipment is considered lost if not delivered within 10 working days of the expected date. The full declared value and shipping fee are refunded.

## 5. Exclusions
Refunds are not issued for delays caused by incorrect address information supplied by the customer, force majeure events, or customs holds on restricted goods.
