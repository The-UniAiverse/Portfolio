# Acme Logistics — Leave Policy (v3, effective 1 April 2026)

## 1. Annual leave
Permanent employees accrue 20 working days of annual leave per calendar year, credited monthly. Unused leave up to 5 days may be carried into the next year and must be used by 31 March. Leave requests must be submitted in the HR portal at least 5 working days in advance for absences of 3 days or more.

## 2. Contractors
Contractors accrue leave at 12 days per year pro-rata to their contracted hours. Contractor leave does not carry over and is not paid out on termination.

## 3. Sick leave
Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year. Absences longer than 2 consecutive days require a medical certificate submitted within 3 working days of return.

## 4. Public holidays
Acme observes the state-gazetted holiday list published each January. Employees required to work on a public holiday receive a compensatory day off within 30 days.

## 5. Parental leave
Maternity leave is 26 weeks paid. Paternity leave is 10 working days paid, to be taken within 8 weeks of the birth.
