# Acme Logistics — AI & Data Security Policy (v1, effective 1 June 2026)

## 1. Approved AI tools
Staff may use the company Claude and Microsoft Copilot accounts. Personal ChatGPT, Gemini or other accounts must not be used with company data.

## 2. Data classification
Public, Internal, Confidential and Restricted. Customer personal data, driver records and unpublished financials are Confidential or Restricted and must not be pasted into any AI tool except the approved enterprise accounts.

## 3. Access
All AI tools require single sign-on. API keys are stored in the vault and rotated every 90 days. Shared keys are prohibited.

## 4. Incident reporting
Suspected data leakage through an AI tool must be reported to security@acme.example within one working day.

## 5. Error codes
Portal error ERR_SVC_4471 indicates a payment gateway timeout; retry after 5 minutes. ERR_AUTH_2210 indicates an expired SSO session; sign in again.
