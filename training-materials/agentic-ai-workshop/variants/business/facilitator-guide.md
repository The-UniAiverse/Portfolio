# Facilitator guide — Agentic AI for Growing Businesses — 1 day
**Audience:** Founders, managers and their key staff (up to 15); little or no technical background  ·  **Duration:** 1 day(s)  ·  **Deck:** `deck-business.pptx` (33 slides, speaker notes on every slide)

## Tone
Direct and practical. Money and hours, not architecture. Say 'this costs ₹X and saves Y hours' constantly.

## Platform
No-code only: NotebookLM for hour one, Dify or n8n for the build. No licences to buy.

## Before the day
- 10 real documents (policies, price lists, FAQs)
- One workflow they hate
- Laptops with a browser
- Run `python ../../demos/run_all.py` on the delivery laptop — it must print ALL DEMOS OK
- Replace `demos/sample_docs/` with the client's documents where allowed

## Run of show
Follow the master run sheet (`../../facilitator-guide.md`) for timing and demo cues, using only the slides in this deck. Variant-specific modules below are inserted where their heading says.

### When NOT to use an agent (most of the time) (15 min, after “safety rails”)
For a 15-person business, an agent is rarely the answer in year one. The wins are: a grounded assistant on your documents, one automated workflow, and everyone using AI tools properly. Agents come when a workflow crosses three systems and nobody wants to babysit it.

### Tools that cost nothing to start (15 min, after “ROI you can defend”)
Walk the free/education tiers: NotebookLM, Dify cloud free, n8n cloud trial, ChatGPT/Claude/Gemini business seats and what they actually cost per month. The $42 bot. Show the automation ROI engine on their workflow.
