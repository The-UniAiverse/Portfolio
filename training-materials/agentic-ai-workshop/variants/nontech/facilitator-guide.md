# Facilitator guide — AI Without Code — Understand it, Use it Safely, Get Value (1 day)
**Audience:** Non-technical professionals from any function or small business owners (up to 20). No prior AI use assumed.  ·  **Duration:** 1 day(s)  ·  **Deck:** `deck-nontech.pptx` (19 slides, speaker notes on every slide)

## Tone
Calm, plain English, one analogy for the whole day. No acronyms without a picture. Long pauses for questions. Confidence over coverage.

## Platform
Whatever they already have: ChatGPT / Claude / Gemini / Copilot in a browser, plus NotebookLM for the documents lab. Nothing to install.

## Before the day
- A laptop with a browser
- Three documents from their own work they are allowed to share
- Their company's AI policy if one exists
- Run `python ../../demos/run_all.py` on the delivery laptop — it must print ALL DEMOS OK
- Replace `demos/sample_docs/` with the client's documents where allowed

## Run of show
Follow the master run sheet (`../../facilitator-guide.md`) for timing and demo cues, using only the slides in this deck. Variant-specific modules below are inserted where their heading says.

### What you may and may not paste in (20 min, after “Demo 2”)
The four boxes: public, internal, confidential, personal data. Which tool is approved for which box. The salary-leak story. Rule: if in doubt, don't.

### Prompts that work, folklore that doesn't (25 min, after “answer is wrong”)
Give context, give the format, give an example, ask for the source. Live: rewrite the same request three ways on their task. Then the folklore list — please, tips, threats — and why none of it does anything.

### Automate one weekly task (40 min, after “Vending machine”)
Each person picks one task they do every week: meeting notes to actions, a report from raw notes, a research brief with sources, a first-draft reply. Build it with saved prompts. Show one to the room.

### Your 5 rules card and what to do Monday (10 min, after “Five things”)
Verify facts · protect data · be specific · give context · you own the output. Monday: use the one automation you built, three times. Then decide what's next.


## Labs in this variant
- **LAB0** — Hour-one win on their own three documents
- **LAB2** — A documents assistant on their files in NotebookLM; 10 real questions; where did it get each answer?
- **LAB5** — One automated weekly task with saved prompts, demonstrated to the room

## Assessment
15-question quiz (AI Aware certificate at 70%) + one demonstrated automation with verification notes

## Done when
Every participant has a documents assistant on their own files, one weekly task automated, and the 5-rules card.
