# Facilitator guide — AI, RAG & Agents — College Placement Bootcamp (1 day) or 2-day with capstone
**Audience:** Diploma / undergraduate students (any stream) in batches of up to 60 with two TAs; placement-focused  ·  **Duration:** 1 day(s)  ·  **Deck:** `deck-college.pptx` (26 slides, speaker notes on every slide)

## Tone
High energy, competitive, hands-on within 15 minutes. Short explanations, long labs. Quiz rounds with a leaderboard from uniAI Quest. Every slide answers 'why does this get me a job'.

## Platform
Free tiers only: NotebookLM / Dify for everyone; Colab notebook for the code-track volunteers. No installs. Works on phones for the quizzes.

## Before the day
- Computer lab or own laptops with browser; phones OK for quizzes
- Wi-Fi tested for 60 devices
- uniAI Quest free accounts created in the first 10 minutes
- Run `python ../../demos/run_all.py` on the delivery laptop — it must print ALL DEMOS OK
- Replace `demos/sample_docs/` with the client's documents where allowed

## Run of show
Follow the master run sheet (`../../facilitator-guide.md`) for timing and demo cues, using only the slides in this deck. Variant-specific modules below are inserted where their heading says.

### What recruiters actually ask (10 min, after “How the two days run”)
Three real interview questions on screen: 'Why does a chatbot make things up?', 'What is RAG?', 'How would you stop an agent from running forever?' — you will be able to answer all three by 5 pm. Set up uniAI Quest on phones now; the leaderboard runs all day.

### Quiz round 1 · AI basics (10 min, after “Demo 2”)
Five questions on uniAI Quest, ai-basics topic, on phones. Leaderboard on the projector. Explain the two most-missed questions.

### Quiz round 2 · RAG (10 min, after “Lab 2”)
Five questions, rag topic. Then one interview challenge (rag-70) answered by a volunteer, room votes, reveal what interviewers listen for.

### Quiz round 3 · Agents + your portfolio (15 min, after “safety rails”)
Five questions, agents topic. Then the portfolio rule: one GitHub repo, README with a diagram, a 10-question test sheet, a cost estimate. That repo is your interview answer. Show the Course Finder: paste your profile, get your path.


## Labs in this variant
- **LAB0** — Hour-one win on the provided sample docs (college handbook / placement FAQ)
- **LAB2** — Everyone: Dify/NotebookLM bot on the sample docs, 10 questions scored. Code-track volunteers: Colab notebook
- **LAB3** — Agent demo walked through together (no-code path); volunteers change one tool
- **LAB5** — Capstone pitch: a RAG bot for the college or a local shop — problem, documents, test questions, cost — 2 minutes per team

## Assessment
Three quiz rounds (leaderboard) + capstone pitch; AI Aware certificate for quiz ≥ 70%

## Done when
Every student has played 3 quiz rounds, built or driven a bot on real documents, and pitched a capstone; top three teams named.
