# Objection bank
## Price — you are more expensive
Acknowledge, then reframe to cost of mis-routes. Proven answer: 'At 6% mis-routes you lose about INR 9 lakh a month; our fee is a third of that and the contract has a 90-day exit.'
## We already have a system
Do not attack the system. Proven answer: 'Keep it. We sit on top and handle the exceptions it flags but cannot resolve — that is where your overtime goes.'
## Security and IT approval
Proven answer: 'We will send our ISO 27001 certificate and DPA today, and we do a 60-minute review with your security team before any data moves.'
## Accuracy guarantee
Never promise 100%. Proven answer: 'No honest vendor guarantees that. We agree a measured target on your own data before you commit to production.'
