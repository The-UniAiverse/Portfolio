# How Kaveri Pharma cut mis-routes by 18%
Kaveri Pharma ships temperature-sensitive medicines to 2,300 pharmacies. In 2025 mis-routed parcels were costing INR 9 lakh a month.
The team introduced an AI address check at label printing and a daily exception report. Within 90 days mis-routes fell by 18% and overtime dropped by a quarter.
The biggest lesson was not the model. It was cleaning 40,000 customer addresses before starting.
Kaveri now reviews the exception report every morning in a 10-minute stand-up.
