# Go-to-market plan — uniAIverse (from v2.6)

Position: **the boutique that ships production AI with Big-4 discipline at a fraction of the price**, proven in public by working demos, a real delivery framework and 25 courses with runnable labs.

## Segments and the offer that opens the door

| Segment | Door-opener (free or cheap) | First paid step | Then |
|---|---|---|---|
| SMEs and startups (Pune/Mumbai/Bengaluru first) | Free readiness snapshot (`/`) + free sandbox demo | Bronze platform (₹15k/month + infra) or a Bronze Delivery Framework engagement (₹2.5k) | Silver when a second use case appears |
| Mid-market, one business unit | Executive briefing (course) or a discovery pack the same day (`/discovery`) | Silver Delivery Framework engagement (₹9k) → Silver platform (₹45k/month + infra) | Gold programme |
| Enterprise / regulated | Board briefing + governance snapshot (`/tools`) | Gold Delivery Framework (₹24k) | Gold platform, customer cloud or on-prem |
| Colleges and universities | uniAI Quest (free tier), Course Finder | Workshop editions (college/university), AI for All | Academy programme |
| Individuals / professionals | Quest, Course Finder | Quest paid tier, one course | Practitioner tracks |

Prices are the placeholders in `pricing/tiers.yaml` and `plans.yaml` until confirmed.

## Channels

1. **The product is the marketing.** Every page on api.uniaiverse.ai is a demo: the audit, the delivery framework, the labs. Link them from every post and proposal.
2. **Two workshops a month** (one corporate, one college) with the working demos; each ends with the readiness snapshot and a Bronze offer.
3. **LinkedIn**: one "lab of the week" post from a course demo output (numbers, not adjectives). Reuse `training-materials/ai-for-marketing/demos/03_repurposing_engine.py` on your own posts.
4. **Partners**: two implementation partners (Sahil's network) who use the Delivery Framework packs as their own scoping tool — the pack carries the uniAIverse name.
5. **Existing network**: 20 named warm accounts, each gets a discovery pack within a day of any conversation.

## Numbers to watch every Monday

Free approvals and pool spend · Bronze tenants active · DF packs generated per week · workshops booked · unanswered bot questions (`/admin`) · projected spend vs budget per tenant (`/platform`).

## What we do not do

No unlimited free tier. No public agent execution. No pricing that hides infrastructure. No promise of "X GPU" — an approved resource allocation up to a capacity.
