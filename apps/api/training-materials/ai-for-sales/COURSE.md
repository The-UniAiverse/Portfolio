# AI for Sales — Workshop Plan (4 hours)
**Promise:** more time selling, less time typing — without sending anything you'd be embarrassed by.

| Module | Min | Content & lab |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Where the hours go | 20 | Map a rep's week; circle the 30-40% that is research, writing, CRM updating |
| 2. Account research in 10 minutes | 40 | Lab: build a research brief on a real prospect (company, triggers, people, risks). Rule: every claim gets a source |
| 3. Outreach that doesn't sound like AI | 45 | Prompt pattern: your voice sample + their trigger + one ask. Lab: 3 variants, pick and edit |
| 4. Discovery call prep & follow-up | 40 | Call-prep sheet; post-call summary → CRM fields → next-step email from a transcript |
| 5. Proposals and objections | 45 | Proposal skeleton from your own past wins; objection-handling bank |
| 6. Guardrails | 20 | Never paste customer contract/PII into public tools; disclosure; pricing stays human |
| 7. Your playbook | 30 | Each rep leaves with 5 saved prompts tied to their pipeline stages |

**Handouts:** prompt library (research, outreach, follow-up, proposal, objection) · data do/don't card · weekly AI routine.
**Metrics to track after:** research time per account, reply rate, CRM completeness.
