# AI for Business Leaders — Trainer's Plan (2 × 4h)
**Audience:** founders, CXOs, function heads. **Promise:** you can pick the right use cases, read an AI proposal critically, and own an initiative to production.

## Day 1 — What to do with AI
| Module | Min | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Cut through the noise | 40 | What LLMs are and aren't; three questions to ask any vendor |
| 2. Where value comes from | 50 | Five value patterns: assist, automate, augment decisions, new products, risk reduction. Case studies per function |
| 3. Use-case prioritisation lab | 60 | Score your own backlog on value × feasibility × risk (the uniAIverse strategy tool) |
| 4. Readiness | 50 | The six dimensions; complete the AI Readiness Audit live; read your own score |
| 5. Build / buy / partner | 40 | Decision tree; total cost of ownership; lock-in |

## Day 2 — How to make it happen
| Module | Min | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 6. Idea → pilot → production | 50 | Gates, exit criteria, "definition of done" for AI |
| 7. Reading a proposal | 40 | Red flags: no eval set, no cost model, no data plan, "agentic" as a selling word |
| 8. Cost & ROI | 50 | Token economics in 15 minutes; the $42/month bot; ROI template |
| 9. Risk & governance | 50 | Risk tiers, the one-page policy, what regulators ask for first |
| 10. Your 90-day plan | 50 | Each leader leaves with a one-page plan: outcome, use case, owner, budget, gate dates |

## Deliverables
Personal readiness score · ranked use-case backlog · 90-day plan · proposal red-flag checklist.
