# AI for Marketing — Workshop Plan (4 hours)
**Promise:** an AI-assisted content pipeline that keeps your brand voice and passes compliance.

| Module | Min | Content & lab |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Content pipeline audit | 25 | Map brief → draft → review → publish → measure; where AI helps, where it hurts |
| 2. Brand voice kit | 45 | Lab: build a voice guide + 3 exemplar pieces; test AI drafts against it |
| 3. Repurposing engine | 40 | One long piece → blog, 5 posts, newsletter, ad variants; consistency check |
| 4. Campaign ideation & audience research | 40 | Personas from real data (not invented); message testing matrix |
| 5. SEO & analytics with AI | 35 | Keyword clustering, briefs, reading GA/Ads reports with AI as analyst — verify numbers |
| 6. Guardrails | 25 | Fact-checking, image/IP rights, disclosure, no fabricated testimonials, regulated claims |
| 7. Build your workflow | 30 | Each team leaves with a documented AI content SOP + review checklist |

**Handouts:** voice-kit template · repurposing prompts · pre-publish AI checklist (facts, rights, disclosure, tone).
